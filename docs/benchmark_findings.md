# Enterprise CLM benchmark findings

## Ironclad

Source: https://ironcladapp.com/product/ai-based-contract-management

Observed public capabilities: end-to-end lifecycle from intake through negotiation and post-signature; no-code workflow automation; native AI for drafting, redlining, clause detection, risk analysis, and insights; deep integrations including Salesforce and Coupa; full contract visibility and centralized reporting data; intelligent tagging for high-volume management; and embedded eSignature with multiple signing methods, approval-history tracking, key-term visibility for internal signers, and signed-agreement capture with full context.

Benchmark implication for CovenX: CovenX has workflow foundations, governed intelligence, redlines, approvals, document storage, signatures, Salesforce/Slack/DocuSign integration foundations, and reporting projections, but still needs a mature no-code workflow designer, production-grade provider adapters, embedded signer experience, intelligent tagging/classification, and deeper post-signature operational analytics to match the public enterprise positioning.

## Icertis

Source: https://www.icertis.com/

Observed public capabilities: an enterprise-wide contract intelligence layer; AI-native drafting, redlining, negotiation, analysis, and agent capabilities; context-driven accuracy trained on large contract corpora; automation bounded by customer controls; and positioning for very large enterprises. The public page emphasizes turning contracts into measurable business impact and presents separate Engage, Operate, and Analyze product areas.

Benchmark implication for CovenX: CovenX has governed AI summaries, extraction, risk findings, citations, Q&A, redlines, negotiation, and dashboard projections, but is not yet equivalent in enterprise maturity around measurable business outcomes, autonomous-but-bounded agents, mature operating analytics, large-scale implementation controls, and customer-grade administration.

## Agiloft

Source: https://www.agiloft.com/platform/contract-management-software

The official page was dynamically sparse in extraction, but Agiloft is presented as a configurable contract-management platform. The benchmark category to verify in product evaluation is no-code configuration and flexible workflow administration, alongside repository, obligation, compliance, reporting, and integration controls.

Benchmark implication for CovenX: CovenX has configurable intake questionnaires, governance entities, workflow models, obligations, integrations foundations, and audit logs, but needs a true visual no-code workflow/policy builder and stronger configuration administration to match this enterprise expectation.

## Sirion

Source: https://www.sirion.ai/

Observed public positioning: AI contract management with solutions organized by enterprise department and industry, including procurement, legal/legal operations, sales, finance, financial services, insurance, automotive, healthcare, pharma, telecom, and other sectors. The site emphasizes enterprise-wide role-specific solutions rather than a legal-only workspace.

Benchmark implication for CovenX: CovenX’s tenant roles and cross-functional workflow model are directionally aligned, but CovenX needs role-specific workspaces, vertical playbooks, supplier/procurement operations, richer post-signature performance and obligation management, and domain-specific analytics to reach comparable enterprise breadth.

## DocuSign CLM

Source: https://www.docusign.com/products/clm

Observed public capabilities: automated internal and external review routing with detailed version control; comments and tasks with email and Slack action notifications; AI-assisted negotiation and summarization; drag-and-drop process design; more than 100 preconfigured workflow steps; prebuilt connectors and rich APIs; a central source of truth; obligation and renewal reporting; keyword, concept, and metadata search; contract analytics; more than 100 pretrained AI models for extracting and reporting contract data and legal topics; and conditional workflow routing based on analytics, risk scores, and contract content.

Benchmark implication for CovenX: CovenX’s workflow, collaboration, Slack/DocuSign foundations, semantic search, AI evidence, obligations, and renewals are directionally aligned, but the product needs a richer process designer, external-review task delivery, email action loops, concept/entity search, model administration, configurable AI extraction schemas, and content/risk-driven routing to reach comparable breadth.

## Agiloft verified feature page

Source: https://www.agiloft.com/platform/contract-management-software

Observed public capabilities: Astra AI for faster drafting, compliance-risk identification, and automatic obligation tracking; end-to-end lifecycle control from request to renewal with visibility after signature; no-code configuration of workflows, approvals, data models, and automations; and searchable, reportable contract intelligence for risk, revenue, and obligations.

Benchmark implication for CovenX: the current CovenX implementation is aligned at the foundation level, but its configuration experience is still UI-light, its obligations/renewals need deeper automation and performance evidence, and its contract intelligence needs more configurable extraction and reporting models.
